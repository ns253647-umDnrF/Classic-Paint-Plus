Local image-format update 2026-11-14:
imageformats.h, imagecodec.cpp, icocodec.cpp, imageoptions.cpp and their resources
add PNG Adam7/palette optimization, BMP 1/4/8/24-bit export, WebP lossless/lossy RGBA,
and multi-image ICO editing. All 30 locales translated. PNG quantization is local
weighted variance partitioning plus refinement, with common-color retention,
alpha endpoint separation and quality/size fallback; it uses no TinyPNG API/code.
Indexed files decode to unrestricted RGBA; exporting never quantizes the document.

only on preview copies. CF_DIBV5 exports standard alpha; legacy CF_DIB uses white.
ICO keeps unedited frames byte-for-byte, asks which frame to edit, and limits output
to 256 pixels. Animated WebP exposes its first frame under a new filename.
Unmodified LodePNG (pinned commit) and libwebp 1.6.0 are statically linked. See
third_party/IMAGE-SOURCES.txt and IMAGE-NOTICES.txt, copied beside the executable.

verification: ../prepare_format_fixtures.py and ../verify_format_outputs.py.
